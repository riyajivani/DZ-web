### PREAMBLE / RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**
1. The Company is engaged in technology solutions, software engineering, enterprise automations, application development, and digital consulting.
2. The Company conducts mentor-guided industrial internship programs (3-month fast-track and 6-month advanced batches) where students participate in live interactive sessions, execute individual live projects, and collaborate on demo group projects.
3. In connection with the Intern’s enrollment and participation in the internship program, the Company will grant the Intern access to certain non-public, proprietary, and business-critical technical and commercial information.
4. To protect the Company’s proprietary interests, intellectual property, client trust, and trade secrets, the Parties agree to enter into this Agreement.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration (including the training, project allocations, and mentorship provided), the Parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

For the purposes of this Agreement, **"Confidential Information"** shall mean any and all non-public, sensitive, or proprietary information, whether technical, operational, or commercial, disclosed directly or indirectly by the Company to the Intern, whether in writing, oral, electronic, visual, or machine-readable format, including but not limited to:

1. **Source Code & Technical Assets:** Source code, object code, system architectures, database schemas, API keys, credentials, algorithms, data structures, repositories, pull requests, frameworks, design patterns, testing suites, bug logs, and server configurations.
2. **Project Data & Client Information:** Real-world client requirements, business specifications, wireframes, prototypes, user journeys, production datasets, operational workflows, customer lists, and client identities.
3. **Internal Materials & Sessions:** Training curricula, session recordings, workshop notes, mentorship guidelines, internal roadmaps, internal discussion boards, and operational procedures.
4. **Business & Financial Information:** Business strategies, pricing structures, marketing initiatives, partnership details, and operational practices.
5. **Derivative Works:** Any code, notes, summaries, diagrams, or deliverables created by the Intern based upon or incorporating the Company's Confidential Information.

---

### 2. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information shall not include information that the Intern can demonstrate through documented proof:
1. Is or becomes publicly known without breach of this Agreement by the Intern.
2. Was already in the rightful possession of the Intern prior to disclosure by the Company, without any obligation of confidentiality.
3. Is independently developed by the Intern without any reference to, access to, or reliance upon the Company's Confidential Information.
4. Is rightfully obtained by the Intern from an independent third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

---

### 3. OBLIGATIONS OF THE INTERN

The Intern explicitly agrees to the following covenants:

1. **Standard of Care:** The Intern shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall apply the highest degree of care (at least reasonable care) to prevent unauthorized disclosure, leakage, copying, or misuse.
2. **Limited & Permitted Purpose:** The Intern shall use Confidential Information **solely and strictly** for the purpose of completing their assigned internship tasks, projects, and guided sessions under the supervision of DZ Infotech mentors.
3. **No Third-Party Disclosure:** The Intern shall not publish, share, transmit, forward, or disclose any Confidential Information to any third party (including friends, classmates, professors, or unauthorized individuals) without prior written authorization from DZ Infotech.
4. **Prohibition of Public Code Repositories:** Under no circumstances shall the Intern push, upload, host, or publish any live project code, client code, proprietary scripts, database schemas, or API credentials to public code-hosting platforms (including public GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Pastebin, Gist, or public Google Drive folders). All code must strictly remain in private company-designated repositories.
5. **No AI Scraping / Public Model Ingestion:** The Intern shall not upload or feed the Company’s or clients’ proprietary source code, credentials, or sensitive business logic into public, unvetted AI tools or third-party web tools that use inputted data for public training.
6. **No Reverse Engineering:** The Intern shall not decompile, reverse-engineer, or attempt to derive underlying algorithms from the Company’s proprietary tools or platforms.

---

### 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (IP) RIGHTS & OWNERSHIP

1. **Company Ownership:** The Intern agrees that all rights, title, interest, and intellectual property (including copyrights, patents, design rights, and trade secrets) in and to:
   - All code, scripts, architectures, documentation, modules, and deliverables created by the Intern during the course of the internship;
   - All contributions made to individual live projects, demo group projects, internal tools, and client workflows,  
   shall belong **exclusively and perpetually to DZ Infotech** (or its designated clients/partners).
2. **Work Made for Hire / Assignment:** To the extent any deliverables or intellectual property created by the Intern do not automatically vest in the Company, the Intern hereby irrevocably and unconditionally assigns and transfers to DZ Infotech all rights, title, and interest therein, worldwide and in perpetuity, without requiring additional compensation.
3. **Portfolio & Resume Disclosure Guidelines:**
   - The Intern **is permitted** to state on their resume, LinkedIn profile, and career profiles that they completed a 3-Month or 6-Month Internship at DZ Infotech, along with high-level descriptions of learning outcomes, general technical skills mastered, and overall responsibilities.
   - The Intern **shall not** disclose proprietary business logic, client identity, live database credentials, exact production URLs (unless authorized in writing), or proprietary codebase architectures in public portfolios.

---

### 5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF PROPRIETARY MATERIALS

1. Upon the conclusion, expiration, or termination of the internship (or at any time upon the Company's written request), the Intern shall immediately:
   - Cease all access to and use of Company tools, servers, accounts, and private repositories.
   - Permanently delete all local copies of proprietary code, project datasets, server keys, and documents stored on personal computers, laptops, hard drives, or cloud storage.
   - Return any physical or digital assets provided by the Company.
2. The Intern shall, if requested by the Company, provide written confirmation certifying full compliance with this section.

---

### 6. DURATION & SURVIVAL OF CONFIDENTIALITY

1. This Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and continues in effect for the entire duration of the Intern’s tenure (3 months or 6 months, as enrolled).
2. The confidentiality obligations set forth herein shall survive the expiration or termination of the internship:
   - **General Confidential Information:** For a period of **three (3) years** from the date of completion or termination.
   - **Trade Secrets & Core Proprietary Source Code:** Shall remain protected **indefinitely** or for the maximum period permitted by applicable law.

---

### 7. BREACH, CONSEQUENCES & REMEDIES

1. The Intern acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement will cause irreparable injury, commercial harm, and reputational loss to DZ Infotech and its clients, for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate.
2. In the event of a breach:
   - The Company shall have the right to seek **injunctive relief**, restraining orders, and specific performance in a court of competent jurisdiction, without prejudice to any other remedies available under law.
   - The Intern’s internship tenure shall be subject to **immediate termination with cause**.
   - The Company reserves the right to **withhold or revoke** the official Certificate of Completion and letters of recommendation.
   - The Company may initiate legal proceedings for financial damages, losses, and legal costs incurred.
